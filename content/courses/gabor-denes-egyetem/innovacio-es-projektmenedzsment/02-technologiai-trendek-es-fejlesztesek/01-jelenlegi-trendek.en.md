## What you will learn

- the most important **technology trends** in computing today,
- what the **IaaS / PaaS / SaaS** cloud models mean,
- how the trends connect and what they mean for a computer engineer.

## What is a technology trend?

A **technology trend** is a direction of development that shapes products, processes and business models **persistently** (for several years) and **broadly** (in many industries). It is not a fashion: a trend is backed by measurable investment, market growth and new standards.

## The most important trends today

### Artificial intelligence (AI)

- **Machine learning**: the system learns rules from data instead of us programming them by hand.
- **Deep learning**: multi-layer neural networks, e.g. image and speech recognition.
- **Generative AI** and **large language models** (LLMs): they produce text, images and code; coding assistants, customer-service chatbots.

### Cloud computing

Resources (servers, storage, software) are rented on demand over the internet, and we **pay for usage**.

| Model    | What do we get?                                   | Example                           |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **IaaS** | infrastructure: virtual machine, network, storage | AWS EC2, Azure Virtual Machines   |
| **PaaS** | runtime platform: we only bring the code          | Heroku, Google App Engine, Vercel |
| **SaaS** | a finished application in the browser             | Gmail, Microsoft 365, Jira        |

### Internet of Things (IoT) and edge computing

Devices fitted with sensors and networking (smart homes, industrial machines, smartwatches) collect data. **Edge computing** processes data close to the device, which reduces latency and network traffic. **5G** connects them with high bandwidth and low latency.

### Cybersecurity

The more devices and data are networked, the larger the attack surface. Trends: **zero trust** – nobody is trusted automatically, every access is verified; multi-factor authentication; AI-based attack detection.

### Data-driven operation and automation

- **Big data** and analytics: decisions are based on measurements.
- **RPA** (robotic process automation): software robots perform repetitive office tasks.
- **Industry 4.0**: connected, data-driven manufacturing (IoT + cloud + AI + robotics), the **digital twin** (a real-time virtual copy of a physical system).

## How do they connect?

The trends reinforce each other: IoT devices collect data → 5G transmits it → the cloud stores it → AI analyses it → cybersecurity protects it. That is why the biggest innovations often come from **combining several trends** (architectural innovation, topic 1).

## Summary

- A technology trend is a persistent, broad direction of development, not a short fashion.
- Main trends today: AI (machine learning, generative AI), cloud (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), IoT + edge + 5G, cybersecurity (zero trust), data-driven operation and automation.
- Trends build on each other; innovation is often their combination.
