---
title: What are programs and algorithms?
summary: An algorithm is a sequence of steps; a program writes it down in a language the computer understands.
---

An **algorithm** is a precise, finite sequence of steps that solves a problem. A recipe is one too: ingredients (input), steps, and the finished dish (output).

A **program** is an algorithm written in a programming language that a computer can execute.

A good algorithm is:

- **unambiguous** – at every step we know what to do,
- **finite** – it eventually stops,
- **general** – it works for more than one specific input.

Example: picking the larger of two numbers.

```python
a = 7
b = 12
if a > b:
    print(a)
else:
    print(b)
```
