## What you will learn

- what makes a task a **project**,
- what the **project triangle** is,
- the **phases** and **roles** of an IT project, and why projects fail.

## What is a project?

A **project** is a one-off activity with a **defined goal**, a **start and end**, and **limited resources** (money, people, time). Building a new web shop is a project; running the web shop day to day is **ongoing operations**, not a project.

IT projects: developing new software, introducing a system at a company (e.g. ERP), moving to the cloud, building a network in a new office.

## The project triangle

```text
                   SCOPE
               (what do we build?)
                     ╱╲
                    ╱  ╲
                   ╱QUAL-╲
                  ╱  ITY  ╲
                 ╱________╲
           TIME               COST
      (by when?)           (for how much?)
```

The three constraints are linked: if the client asks for **more features**, either **more time** or **more money** is needed – or **quality** suffers. A project manager's main job is to keep this balance and to handle changes consciously, agreeing them with the client.

## The project life cycle

| Phase          | Main question and result                                            |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **initiation** | is it worth it? – business case, project charter                    |
| **planning**   | what, when, who, for how much? – task list, schedule, budget, risks |
| **execution**  | doing the work                                                      |
| **monitoring** | are we on plan? – status reports, handling deviations               |
| **closing**    | handover, lessons learned                                           |

## Roles

- **client / sponsor**: funds it and decides the big questions,
- **project manager**: plans, organises, tracks progress, manages risks,
- **project team**: developers, testers, designers,
- **stakeholders**: everyone affected by the project (users, operations, legal).

## Why do IT projects fail?

The most common reasons: vague or constantly changing requirements (**scope creep**), over-optimistic estimates, little communication with the client, not involving the users, and nobody dealing with risks.

## Summary

- Project = one-off, goal-driven, time-bound work with limited resources.
- Project triangle: scope – time – cost, with quality in the middle.
- Phases: initiation, planning, execution, monitoring, closing.
