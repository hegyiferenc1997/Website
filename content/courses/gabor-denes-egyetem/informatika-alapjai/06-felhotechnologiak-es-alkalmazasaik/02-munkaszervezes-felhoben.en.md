## What you will learn

- how a team works **on the same document at the same time**,
- which **communication** and **task management** tools exist,
- how a **Kanban board** works.

## Cloud storage and co-editing

**Cloud storage** (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud) keeps your files on a server and **syncs** them to all your devices. Benefits: nothing is lost if the laptop breaks; reachable from anywhere; **shareable** with a link; old **versions** can be restored.

With **co-editing** (Google Docs, Microsoft 365 Word/Excel online) several people write the same file at once and everyone sees the others' cursors. No more `assignment_final_v3_FIXED.docx`: there is one document, and the **version history** shows who wrote what.

Useful features:

- **comments** and **mentions** (`@name`): a question or task attached to a specific paragraph,
- **suggestion mode** (track changes): a change only goes in once accepted,
- **permissions**: viewer, commenter or editor.

## Team communication

| Tool              | What for?                                                           |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Microsoft Teams   | channels, video calls, Office integration (companies, universities) |
| Slack             | channel chat, many integrations (developer teams)                   |
| Discord           | voice and text channels (communities, student groups)               |
| Zoom, Google Meet | video conferencing                                                  |
| e-mail            | formal, longer, with external partners                              |

Tip: one topic = one channel or thread. Quick questions go to chat, decisions and formal things to e-mail or a document.

## Task management: Kanban board

A **Kanban board** shows the state of tasks in columns. Every task is a card moving from left to right:

```text
┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│  To do       │  In progress │  Done        │
├──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┤
│ [Collect     │ [Write the   │ [Choose the  │
│  sources]    │  intro]      │  topic]      │
│ [Figures]    │              │              │
└──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

Tools: **Trello**, **Microsoft Planner**, **Jira**, **GitHub Projects**, **Notion**, **Asana**. Each card can have an owner, a deadline, labels and a checklist.

Rule: limit how many cards may be in “In progress” at once (**WIP limit**); this way the team finishes what it started.

## Shared calendar

Outlook and Google Calendar show who is free when, so finding a meeting time is easy, and the invitation automatically includes the video call link.

## Summary

- Cloud storage: sync, sharing, versions; co-editing: one document, many authors.
- Communication: Teams, Slack, Discord, video conferencing, e-mail.
- Kanban board: To do → In progress → Done, with a WIP limit.
